Four names, on purpose. The enr_ id is the contributor and outlives everything; the node name is a label; the session is one running copy of the worker and ends with a restart; an attempt is one leased piece of work. That split is why restarting the coordinator loses no points and grants no authority, and why offboarding one machine leaves every other untouched. The hardware and model lines are what the machine says about itself: they decide what it is eligible for, and they are not evidence it has any of it. Nothing here ranks machines.

Points mean a nonempty, attempt-bound worker result was accepted. They do not mean the candidate was selected, that validation passed, or that the output is correct — and they are not money, a token, or a claim on future value.
